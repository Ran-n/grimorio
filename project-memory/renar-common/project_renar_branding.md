# Renar — Name, Etymology, and Brand Philosophy

**Decided:** 2026-07-17. Renar is the product name. (The repo/dir were originally named "xoutaim" as a working name; both renar-frontend and renar-backend were renamed to match on 2026-07-18 — see [[project_apk_build]].)

## What it is

Renar is a universal personal archive: an app for preserving the cultural and personal journey of a life. It is deliberately not named after one medium (film, books, travel, music) — it's the complete record of every meaningful experience a person has, and the connections between them.

Covers, present and future: films & TV, books & comics, video games, YouTube videos, music & albums, podcasts, concerts & festivals, museums, paintings & sculpture, architecture, historical sites, artists & creators, cities & countries, restaurants, exhibitions — any category of human experience.

Its purpose is not tracking consumption. It's preserving culture, curiosity, and memory.

## Core meaning: the journey and the vessel, at once

Renar carries a deliberate dual meaning — this is the key idea that makes the name work, and the reason the second draft supersedes the first:

- Renar is the **journey**: the continuous thread of a life, formed by every book read, film watched, museum visited, city discovered, concert attended, work of art admired, creator encountered.
- Renar is also the **vessel**: the place that records and preserves that journey.

It is the path *and* the archive of the path — not a separate tracker bolted onto a life, but part of how the journey exists at all. A journey only truly exists because it leaves traces; the memories and the record of them are inseparable.

## Reconstructed etymology

Renar is not an attested historical word. It's an original reconstruction inspired by the phonology and naming conventions of ancient Gallaecian (the Celtic language once spoken in Galicia) and broader Proto-Celtic — the same approach used by successful fictional languages and enduring brand names: a believable word with a coherent internal history, not a claim of authenticity.

- **ren** — journey, path, course through life, accumulated experience
- **-ar** — vessel, embodiment, continuity, permanence

Together: *"the living journey and the enduring record of that journey."*

## Brand philosophy

People don't remember life as isolated categories — they remember moments, and moments connect. A museum recalls a city; a song recalls a trip; a book leads to an author; a painting leads to an exhibition. Renar exists to preserve those connections as a complete map of a person's cultural and personal life.

Renar is not a tracker. Not a database. Not a recommendation engine. It is the journey itself, faithfully preserved.

**Brand values:** curiosity, memory, culture, discovery, permanence, knowledge, simplicity, ownership of one's history, lifelong learning.

## Mission & vision

**Mission:** to become humanity's personal archive of culture and experience — the definitive place people preserve everything meaningful they experience throughout life.

**Vision:** the world's most complete personal knowledge and experience archive — a place to look back years later and rediscover the books loved, museums visited, countries explored, artists discovered, and moments that shaped who you became. A lifelong companion.

## Relationship with Breren

Renar exists under the company **Breren**. The two names intentionally belong to the same fictional linguistic family (both reconstructed, both Gallaecian/Proto-Celtic–inspired), and split one philosophy in two:

- **Breren creates** — the builder; makes useful digital tools. Philosophy: *"Build things that genuinely improve people's lives."*
- **Renar preserves** — one of those tools; a lifelong companion that accompanies the journey while becoming its enduring memory.

## Short definition (for bios, store listings, etc.)

> Renar — a reconstructed Gallaecian-inspired name for both the journey of a life and the vessel that preserves it. A lifelong archive of the experiences that define you, under Breren.

## Domain & collision check (2026-07-17, domain updated 2026-07-17)

Domain target: **renar.breren.com** (subdomain of the Breren parent domain) — for now. `renar.app` was considered but not purchased; not the current plan.

Known name collisions, assessed by severity:
- **renar.com** — active US AI/software company ("RENAR", cognitive-bias/expert-knowledge tool). No confirmed USPTO registration found via web search (search doesn't index the USPTO database — run a real query at tmsearch.uspto.gov before filing a mark). Different product category (B2B expert-knowledge tool vs. consumer culture archive) lowers real conflict risk, but this is the one collision worth formally clearing before trademark filing.
- **renar.gob.ar** — Argentina's National Firearms Registry (Registro Nacional de Armas). High-authority government site, Spanish-language — same language market as initial target users. Risk is reputational/SEO on the *bare* term "renar", not legal. Searching "renar app" (rather than bare "renar") does not surface it — once indexed under the app name / app-store listings, this mostly resolves itself. Worth re-checking Google.es / Google.ar rankings for "renar app" close to launch.
- Minor/ignorable: renar.ai (small design agency), renar.site (fashion brand), renar.com.br (furniture retailer), renar.jp (Japanese conglomerate, different market), renar.ro (Romania's accreditation body — B2B/gov niche, unlikely confusion). "Renar" is also an obscure regional Spanish dialect word (Salamanca: "to feed livestock") — irrelevant to brand.

**Decision:** proceeding with Renar / renar.breren.com. `breren.com` is registered/owned. Search-ranking recheck done 2026-07-17: "renar app" / "renar breren" surface no collision (no Argentina RENAR firearms registry, no app-store hit). Repo/package renamed from `xoutaim`/`xoutaim-frontend`/`xoutaim-backend` to `renar`/`renar-frontend`/`renar-backend` on 2026-07-18.
